# hosa27 voice recording script

Record each line in a quiet room, at a calm, slightly slow pace, in a friendly voice.
Save each recording with the exact file name into `app/audio/own/` (`.m4a` from Voice Memos is fine).
Where it says (pause 1 second), pause for about one second between items.

| File name | Say exactly |
|---|---|
| `welcome.m4a` | Welcome. This brain health check takes about seven minutes. There are no wrong answers. Just do your best. First, listen to each question. When you hear a soft chime and see the words Your turn, speak your answer out loud. Tap Start when you are ready. |
| `orient_day.m4a` | What day of the week is it today? |
| `orient_date.m4a` | What is today's date? Please say the number. |
| `orient_month.m4a` | What month is it? |
| `orient_year.m4a` | What year is it? |
| `orient_place.m4a` | What is the name of the place we are in right now? |
| `orient_city.m4a` | What city are we in? |
| `learn_1.m4a` | I am going to say five words. Listen carefully. When I am done, say all the words you remember, in any order.  (pause)  river  (pause 1 second)  candle  (pause 1 second)  jacket  (pause 1 second)  garden  (pause 1 second)  violin |
| `learn_2.m4a` | I will say the same five words again. Please remember them, because I will ask you for them later. When I am done, say all the words you remember.  (pause)  river  (pause 1 second)  candle  (pause 1 second)  jacket  (pause 1 second)  garden  (pause 1 second)  violin |
| `trail_practice.m4a` | Let's practice. Tap the circles in order, going between numbers and letters. Tap one, then A, then two, then B. |
| `trail.m4a` | Now do the same with more circles. Tap one, then A, then two, then B, then three, then C, and keep going until the end. Work as quickly and carefully as you can. |
| `clock.m4a` | Please draw a clock. Put in all the numbers, and set the hands to twenty past eight. Use your finger. Tap Done when you are finished. |
| `name_1.m4a` | What is this? |
| `name_2.m4a` | What is this? |
| `name_3.m4a` | What is this? |
| `digits_5.m4a` | I will say some numbers. When I am done, say them back in the same order.  (pause)  4  (pause 1 second)  9  (pause 1 second)  3  (pause 1 second)  7  (pause 1 second)  1 |
| `digits_6.m4a` | Here are some more numbers. When I am done, say them back in the same order.  (pause)  8  (pause 1 second)  2  (pause 1 second)  6  (pause 1 second)  4  (pause 1 second)  9  (pause 1 second)  3 |
| `letters.m4a` | I will read some letters. Tap the big button every time you hear the letter M. Do not tap for other letters. Get ready. |
| `serial3.m4a` | Start at fifty and take away three. Then keep taking away three from each answer. Say five answers out loud. |
| `sentence_1.m4a` | I will say a sentence. Repeat it exactly as I say it.  (pause)  The tall man carried a red umbrella to the market on Tuesday. |
| `sentence_2.m4a` | Here is another sentence. Repeat it exactly.  (pause)  Grandma baked three loaves of bread before the snowstorm arrived. |
| `fluency.m4a` | Name as many different fruits as you can. You have one minute. Start when you see the timer. |
| `abstract_1.m4a` | How are a piano and a drum alike? |
| `abstract_2.m4a` | How are a ruler and a thermometer alike? |
| `recall.m4a` | Earlier, I said five words and asked you to remember them. Please say all the words you remember. |
| `end.m4a` | All done. Thank you very much. Please hand the tablet back. |
