# hosa27 voice recording script

Record each line in a quiet room, at a calm, slightly slow pace, in a friendly voice.
Save each recording with the exact file name into `app/audio/own/` (`.m4a` from Voice Memos is fine).
Where it says (pause 1 second), pause for about one second between items.

| File name | Say exactly |
|---|---|
| `welcome.m4a` | Welcome. This check takes about seven minutes. There are no wrong answers. Just do your best. Listen to each question. When you hear a chime and see Your turn, say your answer out loud. Tap Start when you are ready. |
| `orient_day.m4a` | What day of the week is it today? |
| `orient_date.m4a` | What is today's date? Please say the number. |
| `orient_month.m4a` | What month is it? |
| `orient_year.m4a` | What year is it? |
| `orient_place.m4a` | What is the name of the place we are in right now? |
| `orient_city.m4a` | What city are we in? |
| `learn_1.m4a` | I will say five words. Listen carefully. When I finish, say all the words you remember. Any order is fine.  (pause)  river  (pause 1 second)  candle  (pause 1 second)  jacket  (pause 1 second)  garden  (pause 1 second)  violin |
| `learn_2.m4a` | I will say the same five words again. Try to remember them. I will ask for them again later. When I finish, say all the words you remember.  (pause)  river  (pause 1 second)  candle  (pause 1 second)  jacket  (pause 1 second)  garden  (pause 1 second)  violin |
| `trail_practice.m4a` | Let's practice. Tap the circles in order. Switch between numbers and letters. Tap one, then A, then two, then B. |
| `trail.m4a` | Now try it with more circles. Tap one, then A, then two, then B, and keep going to the end. Go as quickly and carefully as you can. |
| `clock.m4a` | Please draw a clock with all the numbers. Set the hands to twenty past eight. Use your finger. Tap Done when you are finished. |
| `name_1.m4a` | What is this? |
| `name_2.m4a` | What is this? |
| `name_3.m4a` | What is this? |
| `digits_5.m4a` | I will say some numbers. When I finish, say them back in the same order.  (pause)  4  (pause 1 second)  9  (pause 1 second)  3  (pause 1 second)  7  (pause 1 second)  1 |
| `digits_6.m4a` | Here are some more numbers. When I finish, say them back in the same order.  (pause)  8  (pause 1 second)  2  (pause 1 second)  6  (pause 1 second)  4  (pause 1 second)  9  (pause 1 second)  3 |
| `letters.m4a` | Letters will appear on the screen, one at a time. Tap the big button every time you see the letter M. Do not tap for any other letter. Get ready. |
| `serial3.m4a` | Start at fifty. Take away three. Keep taking away three from each answer. Say five answers out loud. |
| `sentence_1.m4a` | I will say a sentence. Repeat it exactly the way I say it.  (pause)  The tall man carried a red umbrella to the market on Tuesday. |
| `sentence_2.m4a` | Here is another sentence. Repeat it exactly the way I say it.  (pause)  Grandma baked three loaves of bread before the snowstorm arrived. |
| `fluency.m4a` | Name as many different fruits as you can. You have one minute. Start when you see the timer. |
| `abstract_1.m4a` | How are a piano and a drum alike? |
| `abstract_2.m4a` | How are a ruler and a thermometer alike? |
| `recall.m4a` | Earlier, I said five words. Please say all the words you remember. |
| `end.m4a` | All done. Thank you very much. Please hand the tablet back. |
