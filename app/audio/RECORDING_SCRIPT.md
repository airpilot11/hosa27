# hosa27 voice recording script

Record each line in a quiet room, at a calm, slightly slow pace, in a friendly voice.
Save each recording with the exact file name into `app/audio/own/` (`.m4a` from Voice Memos is fine).
Where it says (pause 1 second), pause for about one second between items.

| File name | Say exactly |
|---|---|
| `welcome.m4a` | Welcome. This check takes about ten minutes. Some questions are easy and some are hard. That is normal. Just do your best. Listen to each question. When you hear a chime and see Your turn, say your answer out loud. Tap Start when you are ready. |
| `orient_day.m4a` | What day of the week is it today? |
| `orient_date.m4a` | What is today's date? Please say the number. |
| `orient_month.m4a` | What month is it? |
| `orient_year.m4a` | What year is it? |
| `orient_place.m4a` | What is the name of the place we are in right now? |
| `orient_city.m4a` | What city are we in? |
| `learn_1.m4a` | I will say five words. Listen carefully. When I finish, say all the words you remember. Any order is fine.  (pause)  river  (pause 1 second)  candle  (pause 1 second)  jacket  (pause 1 second)  garden  (pause 1 second)  violin |
| `learn_2.m4a` | I will say the same five words again. Try to remember them. I will ask for them again later. When I finish, say all the words you remember.  (pause)  river  (pause 1 second)  candle  (pause 1 second)  jacket  (pause 1 second)  garden  (pause 1 second)  violin |
| `trail_practice.m4a` | Let's practice. Tap the circles in order. Switch between numbers and letters. Tap one, then A, then two, then B. |
| `trail.m4a` | Now try it with more circles. Tap one, then A, then two, then B, and keep going until you reach six, then F. Go as quickly and carefully as you can. |
| `copy_box.m4a` | Look at the box. Please copy it in the empty drawing space, as carefully as you can. Draw every line you see. Use your finger. Tap Done when you are finished. |
| `rotate_practice.m4a` | Look at the shape at the top. One shape below is the same shape, just turned around. The others are flipped over, like in a mirror. Tap the one that is the same. Let's practice. |
| `rotate_1.m4a` | Now try this one. Tap the shape that is the same as the top shape, just turned. |
| `rotate_2.m4a` | One more. Tap the shape that is the same, just turned. |
| `name_1.m4a` | What is this called? |
| `name_2.m4a` | What is this called? |
| `name_3.m4a` | What is this called? |
| `digits_6.m4a` | I will say some numbers. When I finish, say them back in the same order.  (pause)  7  (pause 1 second)  2  (pause 1 second)  8  (pause 1 second)  5  (pause 1 second)  1  (pause 1 second)  6 |
| `digits_7.m4a` | Now a longer list. When I finish, say the numbers back in the same order.  (pause)  3  (pause 1 second)  9  (pause 1 second)  4  (pause 1 second)  1  (pause 1 second)  8  (pause 1 second)  6  (pause 1 second)  2 |
| `letters.m4a` | Letters will appear on the screen, one at a time. Tap the big button every time you see the letter M. Do not tap for any other letter, even if it looks like M. Get ready. |
| `serial7.m4a` | Start at eighty. Take away seven. Keep taking away seven from each answer. Say five answers out loud. |
| `sentence_1.m4a` | I will say a sentence. Repeat it exactly the way I say it.  (pause)  The woman who lives next to the bakery walks her two dogs every evening. |
| `sentence_2.m4a` | Here is another sentence. Repeat it exactly the way I say it.  (pause)  Before the storm arrived, the farmer locked the barn and counted all of his sheep. |
| `fluency.m4a` | Say as many words as you can that start with the letter P, as in Peter. Do not use names of people or places. You have one minute. Start when you see the timer. |
| `abstract_1.m4a` | How are a newspaper and a radio alike? |
| `abstract_2.m4a` | How are a poem and a painting alike? |
| `recall.m4a` | Earlier, I said five words. Please say all the words you remember. |
| `recognize.m4a` | Here are some words. Tap every word that was in the list of five words from earlier. Then tap Done. |
| `end.m4a` | All done. Thank you very much. Please hand the tablet back. |
